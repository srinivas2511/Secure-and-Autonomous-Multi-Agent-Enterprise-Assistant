# Vendor Contract Guidelines

**Signature authority**: Only the CFO and CEO have general authority to sign contracts on behalf of the company. Department heads may sign contracts up to $25,000 only with a written delegation of authority from the CFO on file. No other employee may execute binding agreements.

**Legal review**: All new vendor contracts must be reviewed by Legal before execution, regardless of dollar value. Standard vendor MSAs (Master Service Agreements) with no modifications may use the expedited review track (2-day turnaround). Non-standard terms, data processing agreements, IP assignment clauses, or indemnification provisions require full review (5–10 business days).

**Preferred contract structure**: Where possible, use the company's standard vendor agreement template rather than vendor-provided paper. Vendor paper typically includes unfavorable IP ownership, limitation of liability, and data handling terms that require extensive negotiation.

**Data processing agreements (DPA)**: Any vendor that processes personal data on behalf of the company must execute a DPA that meets GDPR and applicable state privacy law requirements before data transfer begins. IT Security must approve the vendor's security posture before a DPA is countersigned.

**Key terms to watch**:
- **Auto-renewal clauses**: Must be flagged in the contract summary and added to the renewal calendar with a 60-day notice window before the cancellation deadline.
- **Uncapped liability**: Vendors that refuse to include a mutual limitation of liability require CFO and Legal sign-off.
- **IP ownership**: All work product created by vendors for the company must be classified as work-for-hire or include a full IP assignment. Retain-license-back arrangements require Legal review.
- **Exclusivity**: Any exclusivity commitment requires Board notification.

**Contract repository**: Executed contracts must be uploaded to the Legal contract management system within 5 business days of signing. Contracts stored only in email or local drives are not considered properly executed for audit purposes.

**Renewals and amendments**: Amendments follow the same approval and review process as new contracts. Verbal amendments are not binding.
