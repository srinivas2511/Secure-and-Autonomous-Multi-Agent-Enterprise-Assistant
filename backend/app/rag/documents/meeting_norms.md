# Meeting Norms

**Default meeting length**: Default to 25 or 50 minutes instead of 30 or 60 to allow transition time between back-to-back calls.

**Agenda required**: Every meeting must have a written agenda shared at least 24 hours in advance. Recurring meetings without an agenda may be declined by attendees. A standing agenda saved on the calendar invite counts.

**Required role — facilitator**: Every meeting has a named facilitator responsible for keeping discussion on track, managing time, capturing decisions, and ensuring follow-ups are assigned. For recurring meetings, the facilitator rotates unless a permanent owner is designated.

**Starting and ending on time**: Meetings start at the scheduled time regardless of late arrivals. The last 5 minutes are reserved for summarizing decisions and assigning action items with owners and due dates.

**Action items**: All action items are documented in writing during the meeting (not after). Each item has one owner and a due date. Action items are shared with all attendees within 24 hours via email or the project tool.

**Camera-on norm**: For internal team meetings, cameras are expected to be on unless the organizer specifies otherwise or an employee has communicated a preference. Customer-facing meetings follow the customer's norms.

**Meeting-free days**: See Communication Standards for focus block hours. Additionally, avoid scheduling internal meetings on Fridays after 3 PM.

**Declining meetings**: Employees may decline a meeting if: there is no agenda, the meeting conflicts with a focus block and their attendance is not critical, or they have no clear role. Declining should include a brief reason so the organizer can reschedule or provide materials async.

**After the meeting**: Decisions and key discussion points are documented and shared even if attendees were present. This supports async teammates, creates an audit trail, and allows people to verify their understanding of decisions.
