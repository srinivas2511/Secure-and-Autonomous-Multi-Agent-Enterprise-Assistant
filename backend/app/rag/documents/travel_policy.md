# Travel Policy

**Authorization**: All business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking. International travel additionally requires VP-level approval. Travel should be booked through the company's preferred travel management platform to ensure compliance and consolidated billing.

**Booking lead time**: Book flights at least 14 days in advance where possible to minimize cost. Last-minute bookings (under 7 days) require manager justification noted in the booking request.

**Airfare**: Economy class is standard for all flights under 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval and is permitted for flights over 6 hours or when an employee must be productive immediately upon arrival for a customer or board engagement.

**Accommodation**: Book hotels in the preferred vendor list at negotiated rates. The nightly limit is $200 in standard markets and $275 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore). Exceptions above these limits require Finance approval.

**Ground transportation**: Use public transit, rideshare, or shuttle where practical. Rental cars require manager approval and the company's rental agreement terms; employees must decline collision coverage from the rental company as this is covered under the company's fleet policy.

**Meals and per diem**: Meals are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts. Daily meal limits: $15 breakfast, $20 lunch, $40 dinner. Alcohol is reimbursable only when entertaining a client and is subject to the same receipt and approval requirements as other meals.

**Receipts**: Receipts are required for all individual expenses over $25. Submit expense reports within 15 days of return through the expense management system. Late submissions (over 30 days) may not be reimbursed.

**Loyalty programs**: Employees may keep personal airline and hotel points earned on company travel.

**Extended trips**: Travel lasting more than 5 consecutive business days requires HR notification and may have payroll implications depending on the destination jurisdiction.
