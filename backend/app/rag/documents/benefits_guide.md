# Employee Benefits Guide

**Health insurance**: The company covers 80% of premiums for employee-only coverage and 65% for employee-plus-dependents on all medical plans. Three tiers are available: Standard PPO, High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA, and Premium PPO. Dental and vision are offered as optional add-ons with 50% employer contribution.

**Health Savings Account (HSA)**: Employees enrolled in the HDHP receive an annual employer HSA contribution of $600 (individual) or $1,200 (family). Employee contributions are pre-tax up to IRS limits.

**Retirement — 401(k)**: The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest on a three-year graded schedule: 33% after year one, 67% after year two, 100% after year three. Employees are automatically enrolled at 3% and may adjust contribution rates at any time.

**Life and disability insurance**: All employees receive basic life insurance equal to 1× annual base salary at no cost. Short-term disability covers 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks. Long-term disability covers 60% of base salary after a 90-day elimination period.

**Wellness stipend**: Employees receive $50 per month (paid quarterly) for eligible wellness expenses including gym memberships, fitness equipment, meditation apps, and ergonomic home-office equipment.

**Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: Free, confidential counseling — up to eight sessions per year — for employees and household members. Covers mental health, financial counseling, and legal advice.

**Learning & development**: An annual $1,500 learning budget for courses, certifications, conferences, and books. Tuition reimbursement up to $5,000 per year for pre-approved degree programs relevant to the employee's role.

**Open enrollment**: Benefits elections are made during the annual open-enrollment window each November for the following calendar year. New hires have 30 days from their start date to make initial elections.
