# Communication Standards

**Channel selection**:
- **Instant messaging (Slack/Teams)**: Use for quick questions, informal collaboration, and time-sensitive nudges. Not appropriate for decisions, approvals, or complex discussions that need a record.
- **Email**: Use for formal requests, external communication, decisions requiring documentation, and anything needing a clear paper trail.
- **Project tool (comments/tasks)**: Use for all project-related discussion so context stays with the work.
- **Video call**: Use for sensitive conversations, complex problem-solving, brainstorming, and anything where tone matters.

**Response expectations**:
- Instant messages: same business day; within 2 hours if marked urgent.
- Email: within 1 business day; within 4 hours if marked urgent.
- Out-of-office: Set an OOO auto-reply for absences over one business day; include a backup contact for urgent matters.

**Meeting-free blocks**: Tuesdays and Thursdays 9–11 AM are company-wide focus blocks. Avoid scheduling meetings during these times except for customer-facing calls that cannot be rescheduled.

**Writing standards**: Write clearly and concisely. Lead with your main point; put context and background after. Use bullet points for lists of three or more items. Avoid jargon for cross-functional communications. Spell-check before sending.

**Decision documentation**: Decisions made in meetings or async must be captured in writing — in the meeting notes, the project tool, or a follow-up email — within 24 hours. Verbal-only decisions are not considered final.

**Escalation**: If a request or blocker has not received a response after one reminder and the expected response window has passed, it is appropriate to escalate to the person's manager. Frame escalations factually, not emotionally.

**External communications**: Any external communication that represents a company position (statements to media, social posts on company accounts, responses to RFPs) must be reviewed by the appropriate manager or Communications team before sending.
