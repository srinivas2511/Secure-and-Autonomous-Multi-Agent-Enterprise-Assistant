# Project Management Guidelines

**Project initiation**: Any initiative requiring more than 40 person-hours of effort or involving cross-functional teams must be registered in the project management tool before work begins. Registration requires a project brief: objective, success criteria, key stakeholders, estimated effort, and target completion date.

**Project roles**:
- **Project Owner** — accountable for outcomes; typically a manager or director; approves scope changes.
- **Project Lead** — responsible for day-to-day coordination, status updates, and unblocking the team.
- **Contributors** — team members executing tasks; responsible for updating their own items.
- **Stakeholders** — informed of progress; provide input on requirements and review deliverables.

**Phases and milestones**: Projects are structured around four phases: Discovery, Planning, Execution, and Closeout. Each phase ends with a milestone review. Milestone sign-off from the Project Owner is required before moving to the next phase.

**Scope changes**: Changes that add more than 10% to estimated effort or push the completion date by more than two weeks require a formal scope change request approved by the Project Owner and any affected stakeholders.

**Status reporting**: Project Leads post a weekly status update every Friday by 4 PM covering: progress this week, plan for next week, risks and blockers, and whether the project is on-track, at-risk, or off-track. At-risk and off-track projects require a mitigation plan within 2 business days.

**Risk management**: Risks are logged in the project tool with a probability (High/Med/Low), impact (High/Med/Low), and assigned owner. Risks rated High × High must be escalated to the Project Owner immediately.

**Project closeout**: Within 10 business days of delivery, the Project Lead completes a closeout document covering: objectives achieved, metrics against success criteria, lessons learned, and recommended process improvements. Closeout documents are stored in the project archive.
