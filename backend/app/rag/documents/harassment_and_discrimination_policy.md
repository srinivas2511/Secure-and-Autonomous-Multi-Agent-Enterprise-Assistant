# Harassment and Discrimination Policy

**Commitment**: The company is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and visitors in any work-related setting including remote and virtual environments.

**Prohibited conduct**: The following are strictly prohibited:
- Harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, marital status, sexual orientation, or gender identity.
- Sexual harassment — including unwelcome advances, requests for sexual favors, and verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature that creates a hostile work environment.
- Discriminatory hiring, promotion, compensation, or termination decisions based on any protected characteristic.
- Retaliation against any person who reports a concern or participates in an investigation in good faith.

**Reporting**: Employees are encouraged to report concerns as soon as they occur. Reports may be made to:
- A direct manager (unless that person is involved in the incident)
- Any HR Business Partner
- The anonymous Ethics Hotline (available 24/7; details in the employee portal)

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly. Anonymous reports are investigated to the extent possible.

**Confidentiality**: The company maintains confidentiality to the greatest extent possible consistent with conducting a thorough investigation.

**Consequences**: Substantiated violations result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination, regardless of the seniority of the individual involved.

**No-retaliation guarantee**: Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a serious violation subject to disciplinary action.

**Training**: All employees must complete mandatory harassment prevention training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter.
