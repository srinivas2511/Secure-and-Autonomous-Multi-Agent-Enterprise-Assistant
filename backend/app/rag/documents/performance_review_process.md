# Performance Review Process

**Cycle**: Performance reviews are conducted twice per year — a mid-year check-in in July and a formal annual review in January. All full-time employees participate; contractors follow a separate process defined in their agreements.

**Self-assessment**: Employees complete a self-assessment in the HR portal at least one week before their review meeting. The self-assessment covers goal progress, key accomplishments, areas for development, and career aspirations.

**Manager evaluation**: Managers submit a written evaluation covering goal attainment (40%), core competencies (30%), collaboration and communication (20%), and growth mindset (10%). Ratings follow a five-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Developing, and Unsatisfactory.

**Goal setting**: During the review meeting, manager and employee agree on three to five SMART goals for the next review period. Goals are recorded in the HR portal within five business days of the meeting and serve as the primary basis for the next evaluation.

**Calibration**: Before results are communicated, managers across a department attend a calibration session facilitated by HR to ensure consistent rating standards and identify development opportunities.

**Merit increases**: Compensation adjustments tied to review outcomes take effect on April 1st. Employees rated Exceptional are eligible for increases up to 8%; Exceeds Expectations up to 5%; Meets Expectations up to 3%; Developing employees are not eligible for an increase in the current cycle.

**Appeals**: Employees may request a review appeal within 14 days of receiving their final rating by submitting a written request to HR. An HR Business Partner will facilitate a structured conversation between the employee and their manager's manager.
