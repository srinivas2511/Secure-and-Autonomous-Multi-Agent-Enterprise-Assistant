# Whistleblower Policy

**Purpose**: Provide a safe and confidential mechanism for employees to report suspected illegal activity, serious safety risks, or significant ethical violations without fear of retaliation.

**Scope**: This policy covers reports of: financial fraud or accounting irregularities, bribery or corruption, serious violations of law or regulation, significant safety hazards, and material breaches of company policy that put individuals or the company at significant risk. It is not intended for general workplace grievances, which are handled through HR.

**How to report**:
- **Ethics Hotline**: Anonymous third-party hotline available 24/7 by phone and online. Access details are in the employee portal.
- **HR Director**: Direct reports accepted in person, by email, or in writing.
- **Audit Committee**: For concerns involving senior management or the CFO/CEO, reports may be made directly to the Audit Committee of the Board via the contact listed in the company governance documents.

**Anonymity**: Reports made through the Ethics Hotline are anonymous. The company will not attempt to identify anonymous reporters. Sufficient detail to investigate is helpful, but identity is never required.

**Investigation**: All reports are reviewed within 5 business days. Substantive reports are investigated by an independent party — either internal audit, external counsel, or a combination — depending on the nature and seniority of those involved. The reporter (if not anonymous) is notified of the outcome where disclosure is legally permissible.

**Non-retaliation**: The company strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against an employee who reports a concern in good faith, including dismissal, demotion, harassment, or any adverse employment action. Retaliation is itself a serious violation subject to disciplinary action up to termination.

**Bad-faith reports**: This protection does not extend to knowingly false reports made with intent to harm another employee. Such reports may result in disciplinary action.

**Regulatory rights**: Nothing in this policy limits an employee's right to report concerns directly to government regulators, including the SEC, OSHA, or other applicable agencies.
