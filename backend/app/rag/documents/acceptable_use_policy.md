# Acceptable Use Policy

**Purpose**: This policy governs the use of company-owned or company-managed systems, networks, devices, and data. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company resources.

**Permitted use**: Company technology resources are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities, consume significant bandwidth, or involve prohibited content.

**Prohibited activities**:
- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal content of any kind
- Installing unauthorized software, browser extensions, or scripts on company devices
- Circumventing security controls including VPN bypass, disabling endpoint protection, or using personal email for work data
- Sharing credentials or granting unauthorized third-party access to company systems
- Mining cryptocurrency or running non-work compute workloads on company infrastructure
- Accessing peer-to-peer file-sharing networks from company devices or network
- Using company resources to operate a personal business or for commercial gain outside employment

**Data handling**: Confidential company data must not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage (Google Drive personal, Dropbox personal, etc.), or transmitted via unauthorized channels. Use only approved tools listed in the IT portal.

**Monitoring**: The company reserves the right to monitor, access, and review activity on company-owned systems and networks at any time, without prior notice, to the extent permitted by applicable law. Employees have no expectation of privacy on company systems.

**Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)**: Personal devices used to access company email or systems must have screen lock enabled, be enrolled in mobile device management (MDM), and have disk encryption active. IT can remotely wipe company data from enrolled personal devices.

**Violations**: Breaches of this policy may result in immediate revocation of access and disciplinary action up to termination. Illegal activity is reported to appropriate authorities.
