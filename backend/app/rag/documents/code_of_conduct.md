# Code of Conduct

**Our commitment**: We hold ourselves to high standards of integrity, respect, and accountability in every interaction — with colleagues, customers, partners, and the public.

**Integrity and honesty**: Employees must be truthful in all business dealings. Falsifying records, misrepresenting information to management or customers, or omitting material facts is grounds for immediate termination.

**Conflicts of interest**: Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict — or appear to conflict — with company interests. This includes: outside employment with a competitor, financial interest in a vendor, hiring or managing a family member without HR disclosure. Disclose potential conflicts to HR before they arise.

**Confidentiality**: Employees have access to confidential company information as part of their work. This information must never be shared outside authorized channels, used for personal gain, or disclosed after employment ends. Confidentiality obligations survive termination.

**Fair dealing**: We compete on merit. Employees must not engage in deceptive practices, disparage competitors with false claims, or obtain competitor information through improper means.

**Gifts and entertainment**: Employees may not accept gifts worth more than $100 from a single vendor or business partner in a calendar year. Anything above this threshold must be declined or reported to Finance. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards) are never acceptable regardless of amount.

**Political activity**: The company does not make political contributions. Employees may engage in personal political activity but may not use company resources, time, or name to do so.

**Social media**: Employees are personally responsible for their public statements online. Do not share confidential company information, speak on behalf of the company without authorization, or post content that would embarrass the company or violate this Code.

**Reporting violations**: Employees who become aware of a potential Code violation are expected to report it through their manager, HR, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is a violation of this Code.
