# Procurement Policy

**Purpose**: Ensure all company purchases are authorized, cost-effective, and compliant with financial controls.

**Approval thresholds**:
| Purchase amount | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Manager |
| $501 – $5,000 | Manager + Finance |
| $5,001 – $25,000 | Director + Finance |
| $25,001 – $100,000 | VP + CFO |
| Over $100,000 | CEO + Board notification |

**Purchase requests**: All purchases above $500 must be initiated via a Purchase Request in the finance portal before any commitment is made to a vendor. Submitting a PR after the fact is a policy violation.

**Preferred vendors**: The company maintains a list of preferred vendors negotiated at volume rates. Purchases from preferred vendors do not require additional competitive quotes. The preferred vendor list is maintained in the procurement portal.

**Competitive quotes**: Purchases from non-preferred vendors above $5,000 require at least three competitive quotes documented in the PR. Single-source justifications must be approved by Finance and documented.

**Software and SaaS**: All software purchases, including free-tier tools that may later incur cost or handle company data, must be approved by IT Security before procurement. Shadow IT is prohibited.

**Contracts**: Any vendor contract or agreement must be reviewed by Legal before signing. No employee other than the CFO or CEO has authority to sign contracts on behalf of the company without a specific written delegation of authority.

**Expense reimbursements**: Personal expenditures intended for reimbursement follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy and do not require a PR, but are still subject to manager approval and receipt documentation.

**Conflicts of interest**: Employees must disclose to Finance and their manager any personal or family relationship with a vendor before initiating or participating in a procurement decision involving that vendor.
